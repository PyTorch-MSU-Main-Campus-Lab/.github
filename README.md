# PyTorch Campus Lab - Mindanao State University (Main Campus)

> *"The question is no longer whether AI will transform society. The question is whether we will actively participate in building that future, or simply consume what others create."*

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **PyTorch Philippines Campus Lab at MSU Main Campus**. We are a recognized student organization and tech community dedicated to empowering students to explore Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning collaboratively.

Our mission is simple: **Turn students from AI consumers into AI builders.**

---

## What We Do

As an official chapter under PyTorch Philippines, we focus on hands-on learning and real-world application. Across the semester, our community engages in:

*   **Technical Workshops:** Hands-on labs covering PyTorch Fundamentals, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing (NLP).
*   **Open Source Contributions:** Teaching students how to collaborate on codebases, submit Pull Requests, and build up their GitHub portfolios.
*   **AI/ML Projects:** Building functional AI solutions, competing in local hackathons, and pushing research experiments.
*   **Community Building:** Hosting AI meetups, study groups, and networking sessions for MSU tech students.

## Contributing (How to submit code)

One of our core goals is to help members build their open-source portfolios. Since our `main` branches are protected, you cannot push code directly. Here is the standard workflow:

1. **Fork** the repository to your personal GitHub account.
2. **Clone** your forked repository to your local machine.
3. **Create a new branch** (`git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name`).
4. **Commit your changes** (`git commit -m "feat: Add new Jupyter notebook for NLP"`).
5. **Push to your branch** (`git push origin feature/your-feature-name`).
6. **Open a Pull Request (PR)** on this main repository. Our Deputy of Open Source will review it, approve it, and merge it!

*Read our full [CONTRIBUTING.md](link-to-contributing-md) for detailed guidelines on code formatting and PR standards.*
